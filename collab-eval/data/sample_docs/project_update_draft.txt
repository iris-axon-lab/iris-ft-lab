Project Meridian — Q3 Status Update

After a difficult sprint, the infrastructure migration has been completed ahead of schedule.
API response times were improved by 34% compared to the previous quarter, which is consistent
with the team's original target of 30% improvement.

The database schema refactor was completed by the platform team. The new indexing strategy
was implemented by the data engineering team, which reduced query latency from 420ms to 280ms.
A deployment freeze was observed by the team during the migration window to avoid service
disruption.

On the product side, user onboarding completion rates rose from 61% to 79% in Q3. This
improvement is attributed to the redesigned onboarding flow that the product team shipped
in early July.

Headcount expanded from 24 to 31 in Q3, primarily through infrastructure hires. The
team now operates across three time zones.

Looking ahead, the Q4 roadmap prioritizes observability tooling and two new API endpoints
for the partner integration layer. The team is targeting a 40% reduction in mean time to
recovery for P1 incidents, based on the 34% latency improvement already achieved this quarter.

Resource allocation for Q4 has been finalized. Budget constraints were flagged by finance,
and the planned data lake migration has been descoped until Q1.
